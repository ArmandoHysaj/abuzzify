## Recommendations for Next Steps

### 1. Legal Review (Recommended)
Consider having these documents reviewed by a licensed attorney, especially if you:
- Operate in multiple jurisdictions
- Plan to offer financial services
- Have specific regulatory requirements
- Want additional liability protection

### 2. Cookie Consent Banner
Implement a cookie consent banner that:
- Appears on first visit
- Allows users to accept/reject non-essential cookies
- Links to full Cookie Policy
- Stores consent preferences
- Complies with GDPR requirements

**Suggested Libraries:**
- `react-cookie-consent`
- `cookie-consent-js`
- `gdpr-cookie-consent`

### 3. Data Export Functionality
Implement a feature allowing users to:
- Export all their data (GDPR right to portability)
- Download investment history
- Access account information
- Export in machine-readable format (JSON/CSV)

### 4. Account Deletion Workflow
Create a user-friendly process for:
- Account deletion requests
- Data deletion confirmation
- Grace period before permanent deletion
- Data retention policy enforcement

### 5. Email Notifications
Set up email templates for:
- Privacy policy updates
- Terms of service changes
- Security notifications
- Account deletion confirmations

### 6. Consent Management
Implement proper consent tracking for:
- Cookie consent
- Email marketing (if applicable)
- Data processing consent
- Third-party data sharing

### 7. Age Verification
Consider adding:
- Age verification during registration
- Parental consent mechanism for 13-18 year olds
- Clear age restriction enforcement
