## 🌟 Design Principles Applied

### 1. **Professional & Natural**
- Forest green conveys trust and stability
- Natural color palette inspired by earth tones
- Sophisticated grays for professional appearance

### 2. **High Contrast**
- All text meets WCAG AA standards (4.5:1 minimum)
- Most combinations exceed AAA standards (7:1)
- Excellent readability in both light and dark modes

### 3. **Consistent Hierarchy**
- Primary: `#2d5a3d` (headings, buttons, links)
- Secondary: `#374151` (body text, secondary elements)
- Muted: `#6b7280` (subtle text, borders)
- Clear visual hierarchy throughout the interface

### 4. **Modern & Clean**
- Increased border radius to 8px for modern feel
- Subtle shadows with consistent elevation
- Smooth transitions and hover effects
