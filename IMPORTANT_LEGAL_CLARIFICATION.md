# IMPORTANT LEGAL CLARIFICATION

## Abuzzify Business Model

**Date:** October 11, 2025

---

## 🔴 CRITICAL DISTINCTION 🔴

### What Abuzzify IS:

✅ **A Cryptocurrency Tracking and Analytics Tool**
- Users manually enter investment data for tracking purposes
- Provides calculations and projections based on user-entered data
- Displays market data from third-party APIs
- Offers educational content and news aggregation
- Pure informational platform

### What Abuzzify IS NOT:

❌ **NOT a Financial Services Provider**
- Does NOT buy, sell, or trade cryptocurrencies
- Does NOT process any financial transactions
- Does NOT hold or custody any funds or cryptocurrencies
- Does NOT have access to user exchange accounts or wallets
- Does NOT provide financial advice or recommendations
- Does NOT act as a broker-dealer, exchange, or investment platform

---

## Regulatory Implications

### Why This Matters:

Because Abuzzify does NOT process financial transactions or custody funds, it:

1. **Does NOT require Money Services Business (MSB) registration**
2. **Does NOT require broker-dealer licensing**
3. **Does NOT fall under FinCEN regulations** (as we don't transmit money)
4. **Does NOT require state money transmitter licenses**
5. **Significantly reduces liability** related to financial services
6. **Simplifies compliance requirements**

### What This Means:

Abuzzify operates as a **Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) informational platform**, similar to:
- Portfolio tracking apps (Mint, Personal Capital for crypto)
- Stock market trackers (Yahoo Finance, Google Finance)
- Spreadsheet alternatives for investment tracking

---

## User Data Flow

### How Investment Tracking Works:

1. **User makes investment on external exchange** (Coinbase, Binance, etc.)
2. **User manually enters investment details into Abuzzify** (amount, price, date)
3. **Abuzzify stores this self-reported data** in Firestore
4. **Abuzzify fetches current prices** from CoinLore API
5. **Abuzzify calculates and displays** profit/loss based on user's data

### What Abuzzify NEVER Does:

- ❌ Connect to exchange APIs with user credentials
- ❌ Access user wallets or private keys
- ❌ Execute trades or transactions
- ❌ Transfer funds
- ❌ Hold cryptocurrencies
- ❌ Verify actual investment holdings

## Customer Support Talking Points

### If Users Ask: "Can I buy crypto here?"

**Answer:** "No, Abuzzify is a tracking tool only. You can track investments you make on exchanges like Coinbase, Binance, Kraken, etc. We help you monitor and calculate your portfolio, but you need to buy cryptocurrencies on actual exchanges."

### If Users Ask: "How do you connect to my exchange account?"

**Answer:** "We don't connect to your exchange accounts. All investment data is manually entered by you. This keeps your exchange credentials secure and private. Simply enter your purchase details (amount, price, date) and we'll track it for you."

### If Users Ask: "Is my money safe on Abuzzify?"

**Answer:** "We don't hold any money or cryptocurrencies. We're a tracking tool only. Your actual investments are held on the exchanges where you purchased them. We just help you monitor and calculate your portfolio performance."
